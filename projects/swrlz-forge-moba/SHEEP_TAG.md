Houses occupy **2.4 × 2.4 units**; walls and ramps occupy **2.4 × 6 units**. Fine snap uses readable 0.6-unit major grid lines with 0.15-unit edge ticks; coarse snap uses 3-unit grid lines. The preview disappears when placement ends and is excluded from map saves and collision. Nearby houses snap flush or one 0.6-unit step apart. The default sheep and wolf collision radii are 0.27 and 0.78; wolf artwork is also approximately 15% larger. A narrow opening can admit a sheep while blocking a wolf. Two houses can also close a sheep's exit completely: the engine allows deliberately bad spacing and does not automatically open a route or move the player out of the trap. Overlapping structures, other physical units, the protected pen and no-build zones reject placement without charging. The building’s own sheep may occupy the footprint when a safe side can receive it.

## Record and review a Play run

While stopped, open **Run recorder** and enable **Record Play run**. Recording starts off by default; the setting persists on this device. Choose **Full · every simulation update** for every observed update, or **10 snapshots / second** or **2 snapshots / second** for smaller state logs. Close the panel and use native **Play**, including **Map Creator → Playtest map**. **Simulate** does not record.

The recorder retains the starting map, rules and actual random seed, world positions and heights, object bounds and appearance catalog, camera lens/orientation, body and limb poses, orders, physical traffic, building/economy changes, captures, wisps, rescues and match events. Written observations describe each object's 3D state and nearby objects using X-east/Y-up/negative-Z-north coordinates. Player visibility and camera-frustum membership are separate fields; they are not an occlusion test. State deltas and periodic checkpoints let you inspect captured times without rerunning game logic. Pause freezes simulation time; resume continues the same run. Restart creates a separate run, and victory finalizes its recording once.

Use **Stop** before reviewing or exporting. The final runtime state is sealed before the authored map and camera return. Reopen **Run recorder** and select a finished run, then scrub the timeline, play the recording, drag to orbit its spectator schematic, follow an object or choose **Recorded player camera**. The written 3D observation and timed events change with the playhead. Post-Stop review includes objects hidden from the player; live Play and Pause retain normal fog and block omniscient review/export.

Download **Export JSON** (`.swyrl-run.json`) for structured analysis, **Export text** (`.swyrl-run.txt`) for written spatial/event evidence, or **Export spectator HTML** (`.swyrl-run.html`) for a standalone viewer that works offline. **Open saved JSON** reviews a previous recording without replacing the map. The latest **three finished runs** remain in this tab; download a run to keep it after closing the tab. Preferences and recording history stay outside native project and portable map saves. The viewer draws a schematic from captured states; original scene artwork and rendered video are not exported. Records are not silently truncated, capture errors mark incomplete records, and JSON viewer imports have an explicit 200 MB limit.
