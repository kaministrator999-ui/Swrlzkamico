Tap/click ground to move. Placement with no safe exit is rejected without charging. **Q**, **E** and **F** choose House, Income house and Fortified house. **Cancel** or **Escape** leaves placement. Double tap an owned house to **Upgrade house** or **Sell / clear**; wolves buy **Wolf upgrade** near a merchant using earned bounty.

Pan with **WASD/arrows**, right-drag or Space + drag on desktop, or drag on a phone. Tap the minimap to explore, use **Following** to return to your selected unit, and **＋/−** or the mouse wheel to zoom. **Pause** freezes the match; **Restart** starts cleanly; **Stop** restores the authored map and editor view.

## House maze craft and chase

Fine placement snaps to **0.15 ground units**, with a coarse **3-unit** option. A grid on the ground and fine edge ticks show the building's **full footprint boundary**, including its current rotation: **green** means a valid site and **red** means placement is rejected. Houses occupy **2.4 × 2.4 units**; walls and ramps occupy **2.4 × 6 units**. Fine snap uses readable 0.6-unit major grid lines with 0.15-unit edge ticks; coarse snap uses 3-unit grid lines. The preview disappears when placement ends and is excluded from map saves and collision. Nearby houses snap flush or one 0.6-unit step apart. The default sheep and wolf collision radii are 0.27 and 0.78; wolf artwork is also approximately 15% larger. A narrow opening can admit a sheep while blocking a wolf. Two houses can also close a sheep's exit completely: the engine allows deliberately bad spacing and does not automatically open a route or move the player out of the trap. Overlapping structures, other physical units, the protected pen and no-build zones reject placement without charging. The building’s own sheep may occupy the footprint when a safe side can receive it.

Live sheep receive 1 gold/s. Upgrades spend gold; selling returns half the paid investment. Candidate ownership limit is 28 houses per sheep. Wolves earn bounty by demolition and tags; the rules expose chase speed, strike damage, interval and building health. The default wolf speed/damage are **7.2 units/s and 85 per strike**, versus sheep speed **5.4 units/s**; these are test values, not a permanent balance commitment.

Sheep bots choose formations and escape lanes rather than scattering houses without purpose. Wolf bots prioritize reachable observed sheep over buildings, including when a closer sheep is sealed away. If no observed sheep is reachable, they breach a blocking house and reroute. Sheep and wolves collide with other physical creatures on their floor and use an alternate lane or back out when ground traffic blocks their path. Raised-route traffic waits safely. Wisps and decoys remain intangible. Both use species-aware paths, terrain and building collision. Fog can use shared team vision, individual vision or be off. Hidden enemy locations do not appear in player selection, hunt commands or minimap; AI uses observed movement and last-known clues rather than an unconditional location feed.

Capture removes the victim's houses. A wisp remains in the pen and cannot build. Live allies return through a viable rescue route; the default contact hold is 0.6 seconds and post-rescue protection 3 seconds. Enemy guarding and route timing remain part of the rescue game.
