# §E v11.226 — Astra Wolf visual, skeletal rig and Sheep Tag animation

## Owner request
Improve the appearance of the wolf and give it a proper bone rig and specialized animations like Clover's Sheep Tag clips. Keep the established Astra courier appearance and do not replace the wolf with the obsolete Fen starter.

## Existing source preserved
§E already had Astra's detailed 4K fur, continuous organic skull and body, separately modeled equipment, comet tail, 3D fur locks, and an optimized game wolf with six independent motion pivots, 29 meshes and ~69k triangles. That source and all original assets remain untouched.

## New wolf model
- The Wolf Model starter now loads the same furred Astra that Sheep Tag uses, rather than the historical v11.87 Fen source.
- A matched native detail layer adds expressive brows, swept cheek and neck ruffs, cream bridge and muzzle highlights, sculpted nose, articulated lower jaw, pointed canines and chest fur. Game groups merge the details for a bounded draw-call cost; individual native topology parts are editable in the modeler.
- The modeler carries a new 14-bone parented rest skeleton: root, spine, neck, head, jaw, tail, and eight upper/lower leg joints. All mesh vertices receive four normalized spatial skinning weights through the existing THREE.SkinnedMesh renderer. This is genuine joint-driven deformation rather than changing the model's static source coordinates.
- The touch-sized Wolf Rig & Motion panel offers an animation selector, play/stop and scrub, bone visibility, selectable joints with X/Y/Z sliders and saving/overwriting per-clip pose keys. The native project JSON preserves `wolfRig` and `wolfAnimations`; imported clips and hierarchies are bounded and validated.

## One motion library, two destinations
`runtime/wolf_rig_motions_v11_224.js` is the shared authoritative pose source for Wolf Model preview and the optimized Sheep Tag game wolf.

| Clip | Action |
|---|---|
| Idle | breathing, head and tail awareness |
| Sniff | investigating the ground |
| Stalk | lowered body, cautious steps |
| Walk | alternating four-leg gait |
| Run | faster bound with spine flex |
| Pounce | crouch, spring and land |
| Bite | jaw snap, muzzle recoil |
| Howl | head lift and open jaw |
| Alert | head scanning and poised posture |
| Victory | prouder stance and tail flourish |

Each clip exposes 13 ordered sampled joint keyframes in versioned native JSON. The Sheep Tag runtime uses the existing optimized wolf animation pivots plus a moving jaw, smoothly transitions between activity states and reacts to actual tag/building events. No changes to physics, capture radius, AI/nav, attacks, resources, house timing, multiplayer/LAN state, fog or XR input. Clover's earlier animations remain separate.

## Scope and acceptance
- Append-only source patch: `patches/v11_225_astra_wolf_rig_animations.py` after v11.225 Material & Texture Studio (which follows v11.224 Particle Studio). Existing player saves are not rewritten. The original v11.90 templates and 4K sources remain intact.
- The detailed Astra wolf may be expensive on low-end phones: authoring uses the high-detail editable mesh; Sheep Tag continues to use its merged lightweight game representation. Real-device performance and aesthetics require the owner's Moto/Quest acceptance.
- This is a native pose animation and skinning library, not a full IK solver, speech/howl audio clip or motion-capture implementation.

## Source integrity
Final generated byte/hash receipt will be recorded in `source-manifest.json` and release metadata at sealing. Narrow mathematical, source packaging and mobile visual checks are used; no unrelated Chromium regression suite is required.

## Hands-on acceptance checklist

1. In Model Atelier, open **Wolf Model**. Check that Astra retains blue courier gear, layered fur and comet tail; the new brows, muzzle, mane and jaw are editable parts.
2. Open **Astra · Wolf Rig & Motion** and expand the panel. Try **Howl**, **Run**, and **Bite**. Scrub the playhead, move the head and jaw joints, save a key, and reload the saved model to inspect its clips.
3. Open the **Sheep Tag** starter and play as a wolf. Confirm legs move with the controlled wolf and automatic hunters, the head turns while alert, and the jaw moves during attacks. Stop and restart to check no left-behind animation pose.
4. On Moto G Power and Quest 2, check responsive UI, smoothness, camera stability, no added raycaster or menu overlap, and that wolf animations do not impact building/rescue/fog, navigation, audio or LAN state.

The test matrix is intentionally narrow: source/rig checks and one compact browser smoke do not replace device acceptance.
