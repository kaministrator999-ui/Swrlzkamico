# §C design preview readability

A standalone Chat phone screenshot showed a twelve-step dark proposal as a small cluster of outlines with colliding step numbers. The schematic sized itself against its distance from the origin and reused dark material colors for thin strokes. This made it difficult to review the planned shape before approving the exact native operations.

The schematic now fits the projected bounds of the visible steps in an inset viewport. Outline contrast stays readable with dark materials, and the active geometry is highlighted with one step number and name below the drawing. The review includes side, top, front and 3D blockout views. The slider, expand control and full frozen operation details remain available. The preview is schematic and carries no authority to modify the editor. The exact eligibility checks, approval request and reviewed fields retain their existing behavior.
